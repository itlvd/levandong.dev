Hello, I'm Lê Văn Đông. Welcome to my blog! This blog was created primarily as a place to document what I've learned for easy future reference. However, if it can be helpful to you, I'll be very happy.

You can contribute to any article by clicking the bottom left corner of the post and creating a pull request to help me improve the content. Thank you very much.

The blog is designed with a minimalist and streamlined style, prioritizing page loading speed. I'll limit the use of images in posts, so it might not look very visually appealing. However, I believe my articles provide an excellent reading experience. If you have any questions, you can use the website's search function or try searching on Google with the keyword: "levandong + your topic" (the results might be on the first or second page).
